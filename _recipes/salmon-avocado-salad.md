ingredients:
- 4 cups baby spinach
- 2 tomatoes (chopped)
- 1 avocado (diced)
- 1 cucumber (peeled and sliced)
- 1/4 cup red onion (chopped)
- 2 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 salmon filets
- salt and pepper (to taste)
- 1 recipe lemon vinaigrette
directions:
- Cook Salmon
- Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Season the salmon filets with salt and pepper. Add the salmon filets
  top side down and cook for 4-5 minutes.
- Flip Salmon
- Flip the salmon and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes or until the salmon is mostly opaque, with just a smidge of softness
  still in the middle.
- Add Salad Ingredients
- Divide all of the other salad ingredients between two bowls, then place the cooked salmon on top.
- Drizzle Dressing
- Mix the dressing ingredients together in a small bowl and drizzle on top.
