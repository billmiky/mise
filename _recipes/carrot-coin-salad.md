ingredients:
- 3 tbsp. toasted sesame oil
- 3 tbsp. unseasoned rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp. fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp. honey
- 1 tbsp. white miso paste
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 lb. orange or rainbow carrots, peeled, trimmed
- 2 tbsp. toasted sesame seeds
directions:
- In a large bowl, whisk oil, vinegar, lime juice, honey, and miso; season with salt and pepper.
- Using a mandoline or sharp knife, thinly slice carrots into 1/8"- to 1/16"-thick coins. (You should have about 3 1/2 cups.)
  Transfer to bowl with dressing and toss to coat.
- Transfer salad to a large container with a tight-fitting lid. Refrigerate at least 3 hours and up to 1 week.
- Just before serving, transfer carrots and dressing to a serving bowl; season with salt and pepper, if needed. Top with sesame
  seeds.
