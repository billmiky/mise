ingredients:
- 'Extra-virgin olive oil: About 2 tablespoons'
- 'Brown onion: 1, finely diced'
- 'Fresh ginger: 2.5 cm (1-inch) piece, peeled and finely chopped'
- 'Garlic: 2 cloves, finely chopped'
- 'Vegan Thai green curry paste: 110 g (½ cup)'
- 'Vegetable stock: 1 liter (4 cups)'
- 'Red lentils: 220 g (1 cup)'
- 'White sugar: 1 teaspoon'
- 'Sea salt & black pepper: To taste (plus 1 teaspoon sea salt for simmering)'
- 'Spinach: 2 bunches baby or English spinach (about 400 g), washed well and roughly chopped, including stems'
- 'Fresh herbs: ½ cup basil or coriander leaves, plus extra for serving'
- 'Coconut milk: 1 (400 ml) can'
- 'Lime juice: Juice of ½ lime'
- 'Garnish: A handful of crispy fried shallots'
directions:
- 'Sauté the Aromatics: Heat a large saucepan over medium–high heat. Drizzle with about 2 tablespoons of olive oil, add the
  diced onion, and sauté, stirring constantly, for 2 minutes until softened. Stir in the ginger and garlic and cook for 1
  minute until aromatic.'
- 'Bloom the Curry Paste: Reduce the heat to medium, add the Thai green curry paste, and stir constantly for 2 minutes until
  fragrant'
- 'Simmer the Lentils: Pour in the vegetable stock, then add the red lentils, sugar, and 1 teaspoon of sea salt. Cover the
  pot and cook for 15 minutes, or until the lentils are just soft'
- 'Puree the Green Coconut Base: While the lentils are simmering, combine three-quarters of the chopped spinach, the ½ cup
  of basil or coriander, and the coconut milk in a blender or food processor. Puree for 30–60 seconds until completely smooth
  and bright green'
- 'Combine & Finish: Pour the spinach-coconut puree and lime juice into the soup pot, stirring for 2 minutes until heated
  through. Season to taste with sea salt and black pepper. Turn off the heat and fold in the remaining un-blended spinach
  leaves—the residual heat will wilt them perfectly.'
- 'Garnish & Serve: Divide the soup into warm bowls and top with extra fresh basil or coriander leaves and a generous scatter
  of crispy fried shallots'

Spinach possesses a delicate foliage and has the tendency tomelt away into soupsor stews, easily eclipsed by other ingredients. ButI wanted something more for spinach in this soup – more guts, more distinction. Unapologetically SPINACH. Here, I’ve achieved this with a simple technique– by pureeing most of the spinach before adding it to the soup, it retains all ofits vibrant colour, emphatically tinting the entire dish.  is soup is fortiﬁedby pantrystaples – Thai currypaste, lentils and coconut milk – which makes short work of this big-ﬂavoured, soul-enrichingsoup.If you want something more ﬁlling, you could serve it with a side of rice, sticky rice or rice noodles
To Make It Heartier: Serve over warm white rice, sticky rice, or rice noodles
Vegetable & Ingredient Swaps: You can substitute kale or silverbeet for the spinach, Thai red curry paste for green, and brown lentils, cooked chickpeas, or split peas for the red lentils
