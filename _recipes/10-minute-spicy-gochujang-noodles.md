ingredients:
- 8 oz wide lo mein noodles (boiled as per package instructions)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1/4 cup coconut aminos or low sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp gochujang paste
- 1 tbsp siracha sauce
- 2 cloves grated garlic
- scallions (for garnish)
- sesame seeds (for garnish)
- chili oil (for garnish)
directions:
- Boil lo mein noodles according to package instructions and set aside
- In a bowl, whisk together all the ingredients for the sauce
- In a pan, heat 1 tbsp of sesame oil and add in the noodles
- Stir fry the noodles for a minute and then add in the sauce. Mix thoroughly to coat all the noodles evenly and cook for
  3-4 minutes
- Garnish with desired toppings and serve hot!
