ingredients:
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp hondashi powder
- ¼ cup seaweed, (broken into small pieces)
- 8 oz soft tofu, (drained and cut into ½-inch cubes)
- 3 Tbsp red miso paste, (white miso and yellow miso also work!)
- 3 green onions, (thinly sliced)
- Hot chili oil, (optional, for serving)
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Watercress or microgreens
- Sesame oil
directions:
- Make the broth. Place 4 cups of water in a large pot, then bring to a rapid boil. Add 1 tsp hondashi powder, ¼ cup crumbled
  seaweed, and 8oz cubed tofu. Stir well. Allow the soup to come back up to a boil, then lower the heat to a simmer and cook
  for 5 minutes.
- Add the miso paste. Turn off the heat. Place 3 Tbsp of miso paste in a fine mesh sieve, then lower that into the soup. Use
  a spatula or small whisk to help dissolve the miso paste into the water (then remove the strainer). Finish with thinly sliced
  green onions, then enjoy warm with a little hot chili oil spooned on top!
