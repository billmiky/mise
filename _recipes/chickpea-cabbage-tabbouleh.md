ingredients:
- ½ cup bulgur
- 2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- 1 medium green or Savoy cabbage (about 2 pounds or 9 cups sliced)
- 3 scallions
- 2 lemons (about 6 tablespoons juice)
- ¼ cup olive oil
- ¾ teaspoon warm, ground spice (coriander, allspice, cinnamon)
directions:
- Heat the oven to 400°F. Put ½ cup bulgur in a measuring cup and cover with an inch of boiling water. Let sit until tender
  and the water is mostly absorbed, about 40 minutes.
- Drain and rinse 2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas, then shake very, very dry in the colander. Transfer the chickpeas to a sheet
  pan and drizzle with 3 tablespoons olive oil. Season with S&P and toss to combine. Roast the chickpeas, shaking a few times,
  until dry and bronzed, 25 to 30 minutes. Season with S&P, then turn the oven off and leave the chickpeas in there to keep
  crisping.
- Core and thinly slice 1 medium green or Savoy cabbage (about 2 pounds or 9 cups sliced). Thinly slice 3 scallions. Transfer
  to a large bowl, season with S&P, and squeeze to wilt—as my friend Caroline says, lovingly but firmly like you're massaging
  shoulders. Add the juice from 2 lemons (about 6 tablespoons), ¼ cup olive oil, and ¾ teaspoon warm, ground spice (coriander,
  allspice, cinnamon). Toss to combine.
- When everything's ready, toss the bulgur with the cabbage. Season to taste with S&P and top with chickpeas. (Without the
  chickpeas, the salad will keep for up to 3 days in the fridge.)
