ingredients:
- 150 g carrot, sliced into matchsticks
- 150 g cucumber, sliced into matchsticks
- 1 tbsp caster sugar
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- salt + pepper
- 500 g shiitake mushrooms
- olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled + sliced
- 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce, or tamari for gluten-free
- 1 large baguette, cut into quarters, or gluten-free bread
- 6 tbsp vegan mayo
- 2 tbsp Sriracha sauce
- 1 large handful of fresh coriander
- 1 red chilli
- 4 radishes
- 1 lime
directions:
- Step 1
- Preheat the oven to 180°C fan / 400°F.
- Step 2
- Transfer the carrot and cucumber to a bowl along with the sugar, apple cider vinegar and a pinch of salt. Stir with your
  hands to combine, then set to one side and leave to pickle for at least 45 minutes.
- Step 3
- Use your hands to remove the caps from the mushrooms and finely slice them. Then use your fingers to shred the stalks. Transfer
  all the mushroom to a baking tray, season with salt and pepper, and drizzle with a splash of olive oil. Mix well with your
  hands, then bake for 20 mins.
- Step 4
- Sprinkle the garlic over the mushrooms, along with the hoisin sauce and soy sauce. Use a spoon to combine all the ingredients.
  Bake in the oven for a further 20 minutes or until the mushrooms are cooked through and most of the moisture has evaporated.
- Step 5
- 'Slice each quarter of a baguette open. Pick the leaves off the coriander stalks and finely slice the red chilli and radishes.
  Note: remove the seeds from the chilli if you want to dial down the heat.'
- Step 6
- Prepare the Sriracha mayo by combining the mayo and Sriracha sauce together in a small bowl.
- Step 7
- Build the banh mi by spreading a heaped tablespoon of the Sriracha mayo on the inside of each baguette, then add a handful
  of the pickled veg to each, followed by a generous helping of pulled hoisin mushroom, some slices of radish, a small handful
  of coriander leaves and some chilli slices. Finish with a squeeze of lime juice. Delicious!
