ingredients:
- 2 cups Original Bisquick mix
- 2/3 cup milk
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup fresh or frozen blueberries (thawed and drained)
directions:
- Preheat the oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
- Line 12 regular-size muffin cups with paper liners. Alternatively, you can grease the bottom of each cup with butter or
  vegetable oil.
- In a large bowl, mix together all ingredients (except for the blueberries), just until moistened.
- Very gently stir in the blueberries. Divide the mixture evenly into 12 muffin cups.
- Bake for 13 to 18 minutes, or until golden brown.
