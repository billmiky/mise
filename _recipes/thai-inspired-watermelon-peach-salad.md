ingredients:
- 1/4 c. fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp. sambal oelek
- 1 tsp. finely grated peeled ginger
- 1 tsp. fish sauce
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1/2 serrano chile, thinly sliced
- 1/2 c. thinly sliced fresh Thai or Italian basil leaves, plus whole leaves for serving
- 6 c. seedless watermelon chunks or melon balls
- 2 ripe peaches, seeded and cut into 1/2" slices
- 1/2 c. dry roasted peanuts, crushed, plus more for serving
directions:
- In a large bowl, whisk lime juice, sambal oelek, ginger, and fish sauce. Stir in scallions, chile, and basil.
- Add watermelon, peaches, and peanuts and toss to coat. Sprinkle with more basil and peanuts and serve immediately.
