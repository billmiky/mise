ingredients:
- 1 watermelon radish, thinly sliced
- 1 candy striped beet, thinly sliced
- 1 yellow beet, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup walnuts, toasted and chopped
- 3 small Radishes, thinly sliced
- A small handful mint
- 1/2 Parmesan cheese, shaved
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- Olive oil, as needed
- Kosher salt
- Black pepper
directions:
- Step 1
- Layer the radishes and beets on a large plate and top with walnuts, cheese, mint, lemon juice and zest. Drizzle with olive
  oil and season with salt and pepper.
