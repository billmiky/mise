ingredients:
- 3 cups rolled oats
- 3 large egg whites
- 2 cups unsweetened coconut flakes or shredded coconut
- 1 cup chopped pistachios, almonds, walnuts, or pecans
- ½ cup millet, quinoa, or white sesame seeds
- ½ cup maple syrup, honey, or agave
- ⅓ cup coconut oil or olive oil
- 1½ teaspoons ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
directions:
- Preheat the oven to 325°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Combine the oats, egg whites, coconut flakes, pistachios, millet, maple syrup, coconut oil, turmeric, cinnamon, and salt
  in a medium bowl, and toss to mix until everything is evenly coated.
- Spread the mixture onto the prepared baking sheet and bake, stirring every 15 minutes or so, until everything is golden
  brown and crisped, 50 to 60 minutes.
- Let cool completely and break any large clumps into smaller pieces before storing in glass jars or ziplock bags.

I want my granola to be lightly (very lightly) sweetened, vaguely spiced, and really, really crunchy. This one is, thanks to things like coconut flakes, millet, and some egg whites, which are there to keep things crisp and light.

This recipe is super flexible. Think of it as a general guideline for texture and flavor when making granola, which is why there are so many options offered. The point is that granola should be a good balance of oats (about half) and then a mix of other wonderful, crunchy, flavorful things like nuts, seeds, and uncooked grains. If dried fruit is your thing, add some chopped dried cherries, dates, or apricots at the end for a chewier texture.

DO AHEAD: Granola can be made 1 week ahead and stored in an airtight container at room temperature.
