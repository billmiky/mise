ingredients:
- 2 sheets nori (dried seaweed)
- 2 tbsp. toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp. kosher salt
- 1 tbsp. togarashi chile seasoning
- 8 c. popcorn (from about 1/4 c. unpopped)
- 4 tbsp. unsalted butter, melted
directions:
- In a food processor, pulse nori, sesame seeds, salt, and togarashi to a fine powder.
- In a large bowl, toss popcorn and butter. Add nori mixture and toss again to combine.
