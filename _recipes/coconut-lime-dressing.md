ingredients:
- 6 tablespoons unsweetened coconut milk
- 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 5 teaspoons fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon finely grated shallot
- 1 tablespoon light brown sugar
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
directions:
- 'Whisk coconut milk, lime juice, fish sauce, shallot, and brown sugar in a small bowl until smooth. Gradually add oil, whisking
  constantly until emulsified. Do ahead: Dressing can be made 1 day ahead. Cover and chill.'
