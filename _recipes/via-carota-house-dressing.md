ingredients:
- 1 large shallot, very finely diced
- 3 tbsp + 1 tsp aged sherry vinegar
- 1 tbsp warm water
- 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1½ tsp honey
- 1½ tsp Dijon mustard
- 1½ tsp whole-grain mustard
- 2 sprigs thyme, leaves picked and finely chopped (approx. ½ tsp)
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
directions:
- 'Rinse the Shallot: Place the diced shallot in a fine-mesh sieve and quickly rinse with cold water. Allow to drain well.'
- 'Macerate: Place the drained shallot in a medium bowl. Add the vinegar and warm water. Let the mixture sit for 2 minutes
  to mellow the flavors.'
- 'Whisk to Emulsify: Whisk in the oil, honey, both mustards, thyme, garlic, salt, and pepper until fully combined and emulsified.'
- 'Season & Store: Taste and adjust salt and vinegar as needed. Cover and refrigerate remaining dressing for up to 1 week.'

Quick Note: The warm water in this recipe is essential—it makes the vinegar "more palatable" and less aggressive.

Storage: Up to 1 week.

3 Ways to Use This Dressing:
1. Crunchy & Refreshing Green Salad. Base: Little Gems or baby lettuces. Mix-ins: Thinly shaved fennel, tender dill fronds, whole cilantro and parsley leaves, and 1-inch pieces of chives. Finish: Toss with dressing and season with salt and pepper.
2. Autumn Chicory Salad. Base: Radicchio, torn into bite-size pieces. Mix-ins: Crumbled Parmesan or Roquefort, toasted walnuts or pecans, and sliced seasonal fruit (pears, persimmons, or apples). Finish: Toss with dressing and season with salt and pepper.
3. Simple Boiled Vegetables. Base: Just-boiled broccoli, broccolini, carrots, cauliflower, or string beans. Finish: Generously drizzle with dressing and garnish with flaky salt.
