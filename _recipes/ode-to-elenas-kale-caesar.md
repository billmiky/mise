ingredients:
- 'The Roasted-Garlic Tahini Vinaigrette:'
- 11 garlic cloves (10 for roasting, 1 for grating)
- 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (plus extra for drizzling)
- 2 Tbsp tahini (look for runny, high-quality tahini)
- ¼ cup red wine vinegar
- ½ to 1 tsp sea salt (adjust to taste)
- 'The Greens:'
- 1 bunch (6 oz) Lacinato kale (leaves torn, stems removed)
- 2 cups (4 oz) curly kale (leaves torn)
- ½ small head radicchio (cored and torn)
- 'The Finishing Touches:'
- 2 Tbsp nutritional yeast (or grated hard cheese)
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Sea salt & cracked black pepper
directions:
- 'Step 1, Roast the garlic: Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Take 10 garlic cloves and slice off the pointy tips. Place them
  on foil, drizzle with oil, and wrap tightly. Roast for 35–40 minutes until tender and oozy. Let cool.'
- 'Step 2, Create the emulsion: Squeeze the cooled roasted garlic into a bowl. The manual method: Mash with a fork until it
  becomes a paste. Add the tahini, vinegar, salt, and olive oil. Grate the final raw garlic clove into the mix. Whisk until
  smooth (add 2 Tbsp water to thin as needed). The shortcut: Throw all vinaigrette ingredients into a small blender or food
  processor and blitz until creamy.'
- 'Step 3, Massage the greens: In a wide, shallow bowl, combine both kales and the radicchio. Pour over the vinaigrette. Add
  the nutritional yeast, lemon juice, and a final drizzle of olive oil. Pro tip: Use your hands to "massage" the dressing
  into the kale for 1–2 minutes. This breaks down the tough fibers and makes the kale tender.'
- 'Step 4, Season & serve: Season generously with salt and pepper. Serve immediately, perhaps with a few extra cracks of black
  pepper on top.'

Roasted-Garlic & Tahini Kale Caesar. Inspired by: Elena in Saint-Henri, Montréal. Profile: Acidic / Salty / Complex / Creamy. Dietary: Vegan & Gluten-Free.

The Profile: This salad is a recreation of the legendary kale Caesar at Elena in Montreal. The "magic" lies in the dual-garlic approach: roasted garlic for a mellow, jammy sweetness and raw garlic for a sharp, spicy bite. The tahini provides a luxurious creaminess without the need for dairy or eggs.

Substitutions & Tips:
The greens: If you can't find Lacinato, use any variety of kale. Swap radicchio for endive or arugula if you prefer a different bitter note.
The saltiness: This dressing is meant to be punchy. If it feels too sharp, add a teaspoon of maple syrup or honey to balance the acidity.
