ingredients:
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 2 heaping teaspoons Dijon mustard
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil, more to taste
directions:
- In a container with a tight lid, preferably a glass jar, combine shallot, vinegar and mustard. Close tightly and shake well
  to mix. Add salt and pepper, and shake again.
- Add olive oil ⅓ cup at a time, shaking very well after each addition, until smooth and emulsified (you may want to wrap
  a kitchen towel around the container, just in case). Taste and add more olive oil, ¼ cup at a time, if dressing is too tart.
- Use immediately or refrigerate up to 3 weeks. The chilled olive oil will form a lump, so remove from refrigerator 30 minutes
  before using to allow it to liquefy. Shake well before serving to re-emulsify.
